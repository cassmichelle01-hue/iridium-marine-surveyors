South Florida marine surveying

Surveying support in Miami

Miami offers a wide range of vessels, from recreational boats to large motor yachts. A survey scope should reflect the vessel’s age, construction, equipment, and intended use.

Iridium Marine Group provides pre-purchase, condition and valuation (including insurance), damage, sea trial, and out-of-water hull and bottom inspections as appropriate to the assignment. Survey scheduling and scope are confirmed directly with the IMG team.

What a marine survey can cover

Pre-purchase and valuation

Document visible condition, equipment, and relevant findings for a prospective purchase or valuation request.

Hull, machinery and sea trial

Where included in the agreed scope, inspections may include a haul-out, accessible machinery, and an operational sea trial.

For vessels in Miami, contact the team to discuss the vessel location, access arrangements, and survey requirements.
